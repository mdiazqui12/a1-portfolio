About me

The short version is on the home page. This is the longer story of how I got here and what I care about.

My story

  1. Mexico

    I was born and raised in Mexico, where I grew up speaking spanish in Guadalajara Jalisco. I have always been interested in technology, ever since playing computer games in my kindergarden computer class, but never had the means to pursue this passion of mine.

  2. New Hampshire

    I moved to New Hampshire when I was 10 years old, where I learned English and became fluent in just two years. I continued to explore different passions and clubs, such as art, music, sports, gastronomy, technology, wood working, and more. In middle school, I vividly remember seeing a computer science club, without knowing what it was really about or how to get involved. I was fascinated by the idea of creating something from scratch. When I showed up, I was surprised to find that the club was a girls only club, to later understand that it was "Girls Who Code". My interest for technology never faded but went dormant after finding out that there was no coding club for boys.

  3. California

    I moved to California my freshman year of high school, where I was able to explore my passion for technology and computer science. I took my first programming class in high school, where I learned the basics of programming and problem solving. This was an important step in my journey, as is what led me to pursue a degree in Informatics & Business Information Management at UC Irvine. I am excited to continue learning and growing in this field, and to see where my passion for technology will take me in the future.

  4. High School

    I began my college path at Elsinore High School, where I figured out what I wanted to study and built the habits I still use today: show up prepared, ask questions, and finish what I start.

  5. Leading a team at Domino's

    Working in management at Domino's Pizza taught me more about people than any classroom. Busy nights meant making fast decisions, keeping a team motivated, and staying friendly with customers when things got stressful.

  6. Transferring to UC Irvine

    I transferred to UCI to study Informatics, with a double major in Business Information Management. I liked that the major sits between technology and people, because the best tools are the ones that help those on the daily.

  7. Learning to build

    I started my capstone with no React experience and ended up leading the front end of a medical image viewer. Since then I've kept building, from data visualizations in R to a prototype dashboard for UCI students.

  8. Where I am now

    I'm a junior, graduating in Spring 2028, and working as a Parking Enforcement Officer at UCI while I look for internships. Being multilingual in English, Spanish, and Italian also helps me connect with a wider range of people.
